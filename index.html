How Professionals Type: The 5 Golden Rules

Rule 1: Never, Ever Look Down

The instant you look down at your keyboard, you break muscle memory formation. Rely completely on the raised physical bumps on F and J to position your index fingers without looking.

Rule 2: Respect Strict Finger Boundaries

Every single key on the keyboard belongs to exactly one finger. Use the pinky for outer modifiers (Shift, Caps, Enter) and never reach across with the wrong hand or finger.

Rule 3: Master the Opposite Shift Key

When typing a capital letter with your left hand, hold the Right Shift with your right pinky. When typing a capital letter with your right hand, hold the Left Shift with your left pinky. Never contort one hand to hold Shift and hit the letter simultaneously!

Rule 4: Rhythm Over Raw Speed

Professional typists do not burst fast and stall on hard words; they type with a metronome-like cadence. Aim for 97%+ accuracy first. Speed is a natural byproduct of accuracy, never the other way around.

Rule 5: Ergonomics & Float Your Wrists

Keep your wrists straight and slightly floating, not anchored heavily into the desk edge. Keep your elbows at roughly 90 degrees and shoulders relaxed.
